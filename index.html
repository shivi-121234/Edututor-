Our Story

I am Shivali Varma in Grade 11 and a student of Deens Academy, Bangalore.

While talking to classmates and friends, I realized that many students struggle to understand concepts in traditional classrooms. Lessons often move too fast, doubts stay unanswered, and online videos can feel confusing or impersonal. I wanted to create a space where learning feels more comfortable, relatable, and less stressful.

That idea became Edututor — a peer-to-peer learning platform where students can learn from other students through interactive online sessions. By making learning more personal and engaging, Edututor helps students gain confidence, understand concepts better, and enjoy learning again.
